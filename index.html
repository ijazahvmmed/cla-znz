Who it speaks to

Most visible: projects, construction, indoor greenery
Two services only, then a completed-projects gallery that mixes villa gardens with office greenery.
Gamlaa's calm, premium feel, but clearly different
The same calm pace, with a deeper, quieter green than Gamlaa's, no lime, no rounded cards. Stone, deep green and a single metal accent, with square edges.
A mix of light and dark
The dark is a deep green, not black. Option A is light with deep green bands. Option B is deep green with light sections.
Luxurious, very simple and clean. Not too plain, not too flashy
Large elegant headings, generous space, one accent colour. Movement is smooth and purposeful, and nothing blinks, spins or pops.
Elegant headings with easy-to-read text
A serif for headings and a plain sans-serif for everything you read at length.
Real photos mixed with designed graphics
